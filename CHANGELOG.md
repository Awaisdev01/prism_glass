## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native Liquid Glass (`UIGlassEffect`) on iOS 26+ via platform view.
- Fragment-shader fallback (`shaders/glass.iplr`) for Android, web, desktop and iOS < 26.
- `LiquidGlassSurface` widget with `GlassStyle.regular/.clear`, interactive glass, tint and corner radius.
- `GlassController` for live property updates.
